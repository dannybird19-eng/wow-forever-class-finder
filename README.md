WoW Forever Class Finder

Welcome to the WoW Forever Class Finder! 🎮

Not sure which class to play? This quiz helps you find a class that matches your preferred playstyle, whether you love PvP, enjoy solo levelling, want to support a group, or simply want to master something challenging.

🧭 How to use the class finder

Start the quiz and answer each question honestly.

Choose what sounds fun to you, rather than what you think is strongest.

Review your results. Your top match is the class that best fits your answers, with alternative matches if you want more options.

Read the strengths and trade-offs before making your decision.

Try the class in-game if you're still unsure!

There are no right or wrong answers. The aim is to find a class you'll enjoy playing for the long haul.

🏆 How should I choose my result?

If PvP is your main goal: Think about whether you enjoy stealth and control, ranged spellcasting, or direct combat.

If you mostly play solo: Consider how much you value self-sufficiency, pets, survivability, and downtime between fights.

If you want to raid or run dungeons: Think about whether you want to deal damage, tank, heal, or bring useful support.

If you want one character with options: Hybrid classes such as Druid, Paladin, and Shaman offer different ways to play.

If you're still undecided: Compare your top result with the alternatives. A close second might suit you just as well.

💡 A few things to remember

Playstyle matters more than chasing the meta. Balance changes can shift which classes perform best.

Your favourite class isn't always the easiest class. A challenge can be rewarding if you enjoy mastering it.

PvE and PvP can favour different strengths. Consider which content you'll spend the most time doing.

The quiz is a guide, not a rule. You can enjoy any class, even if it isn't your top match.

Have fun!

The best class is the one you look forward to logging in and playing. Use your results as a starting point, explore your options, and find your own way to play WoW Forever.

Good luck, and see you in Azeroth! ⚔️
